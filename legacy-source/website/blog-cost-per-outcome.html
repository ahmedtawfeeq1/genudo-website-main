Stop measuring tokens. Measure cost per outcome.

YEYasser El-SayedJun 21, 20265 min read
Cost per outcome

Token pricing is a trap for planning. It tells you what a request cost, not whether it was worth it. The number that actually runs your business is cost per outcome: what you paid to resolve a ticket, qualify a lead, or book a meeting.

Why one model can’t win

Pick a cheap model and you’ll fumble the hard cases that matter most. Pick an expensive one and you’ll pay reasoning-model prices to answer “what are your opening hours?” Neither is a strategy.

Routing changes the math

When a fast model handles the routine 80% and a reasoning model takes the tricky 20%, your blended cost per outcome drops sharply while quality on the hard cases goes up. A rough shape we see often:

  • Fast model, routine reply: fractions of a cent.
  • Reasoning model, complex case: 10–20× more — but rare.
  • Blended cost per resolved conversation: closer to the cheap end than the expensive one.
Optimise the blend, not the model.

What to actually track

Put three numbers on the wall: cost per resolved conversation, resolution rate, and the share of traffic that needed the expensive model. When routing is working, the first two improve while the third stays small. That’s the flywheel — and it’s exactly what GenuDo’s model routing and Analytics Center are built to show you.
