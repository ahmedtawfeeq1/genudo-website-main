Introducing the Analytics Center

YEYasser El-SayedMay 30, 20264 min read
New: Analytics Center

Today we’re shipping the Analytics Center: one place to see what your entire AI workforce is producing.

As teams add employees — a Sales Agent, a Customer Service agent, an Operations Assistant — the question shifts from “does it reply?” to “what is it producing, and what does it cost?” The Analytics Center answers that.

What you can see

  • Outcomes — meetings booked, tickets resolved, deals advanced, by pipeline stage.
  • Workload — volume and response times per channel and per agent.
  • Cost — cost per conversation and cost per outcome, with model routing factored in.
  • Comparisons — filter by agent to see who’s carrying which load.
If you can measure an employee, you can manage one.

Available now

The Analytics Center is live for all workspaces. Open the dashboard, and your existing agents’ history is already there. Pair it with model routing to watch your blended cost per outcome fall as the mix improves.
