Can we run a separate agent per client?

Yes — that’s the model. Each client gets its own tuned employee with its own offers, tone, channels and scoring, all managed from one agency workspace.

Absolutely. You configure persona, tone and language per client, so every brand’s audience gets an on-brand experience.

Can we show clients the results?

Every lead, conversion and cost is tracked per client, and GENU posts wraps to each account channel — the ROI story is ready to share.

Does it plug into our clients’ CRMs?

Yes. The Operations Assistant syncs leads and outcomes to each client’s CRM and tools. See the integrations page for details.
