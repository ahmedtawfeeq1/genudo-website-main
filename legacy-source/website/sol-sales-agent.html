Questions

Good to know

Will it sound like a robot to my leads?

No. You shape its persona, tone and language — including Arabic and English — and it answers from your knowledge base, not generic scripts. Most leads never realise they started with an agent.

Can a human take over mid-conversation?

Any time. Reps can jump into any chat from the inbox, and you can set rules so high-value or sensitive conversations are handed to a person automatically.

How does it decide a lead is qualified?

You define the qualifying questions and the scoring. GENU asks them naturally in conversation, weighs the answers against your ICP, and only routes the ones that clear your bar.

Which channels and CRMs does it work with?

WhatsApp, Instagram, webchat and email out of the box, and it writes back to HubSpot, Zoho and others. See the integrations page for the full list.
