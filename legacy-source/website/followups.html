The concept

Most leads don’t say no — they go quiet.

The money is in the follow-up, and it’s exactly what busy teams drop. GenuDo attaches a follow-up sequence to each stage, so every lead gets a timely, human, on-context nudge — and the ones who never reply get moved out of the way automatically.

Sequences

A cadence for every stage.

Each stage carries its own sequence — a fresh lead gets chased faster than a warm proposal. Set how many follow-ups and how long to wait between each; the agent spaces them out and stops the moment the lead replies.

  • Multiple messages per sequence
  • Per-step wait — hours or days
  • Stops automatically on reply

No-answer action

Silence is an outcome, handled.

When the sequence runs out and there’s still no reply, the agent moves the opportunity to the stage you choose — usually a No Answer stage — so your live pipeline only ever shows leads worth chasing.

  • Pick the destination stage
  • No dead leads clogging the board
  • Re-engage later from one place

Guardrails

Controlled by instructions and assets.

Follow-ups aren’t random pings. Each one obeys your written rules and pulls from a library of assets built to catch attention — a video, a case study, a key benefit — chosen for the lead in front of it.

Follow-up InstructionsRules each follow-up should follow…
############################################## ## FOLLOW-UP INSTRUCTIONS — STAGE: ENGAGED ############################################## —— 1. GENERAL RULES —— • Parse full history; never repeat content verbatim. • Polite, concise, human: address the lead by name. • One clear ask per message — no walls of text. • Match the lead’s language (Arabic / English). —— 2. TONE —— • Helpful, not pushy. Curious about their need.
