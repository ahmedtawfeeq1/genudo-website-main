The controls

Three layers, working together.

Data & privacy

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Automatic PII detection & masking before model calls
  • Configurable data residency (EU & Middle East regions)
  • Your data is never used to train shared models
  • Configurable retention & one-click deletion

Access & controls

  • Role-based access control for every workspace member
  • SSO & SAML for enterprise identity providers
  • Scoped tool & knowledge access per agent
  • Human-in-the-loop approvals for high-stakes actions
  • API keys you can scope, rotate and revoke

Monitoring & oversight

  • Immutable audit log of every action and result
  • Full agent tracing — see every step of every run
  • Real-time monitoring and alerting
  • Cost visibility per agent, action and outcome
  • Take over any conversation instantly

Questions

Security FAQ

Do you use our data to train models?

No. Your conversations, knowledge and customer data are never used to train shared or third-party models. Your data is yours, used only to run your agents.

Where is our data stored?

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Enterprise plans can pin data residency to EU or Middle East regions to meet local requirements.

Can we control what an agent is allowed to do?

Completely. You scope each agent’s knowledge and tools, set human-in-the-loop approval gates for high-stakes actions, and can take over any conversation instantly.

How do we get your compliance reports?

Request our security pack for the SOC 2 Type II report, our DPA, sub-processor list and architecture overview. Contact us and we’ll share it under NDA.
