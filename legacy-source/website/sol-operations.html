The concept

It’s the teammate that lives between your tools.

Most operations work is moving information from one system to another and chasing people to act. The Operations Assistant does exactly that — triggered by your events, following your rules, on your tools.

  • Triggers workflows from any event or signal
  • Keeps records in sync across CRM, sheets and tools
  • Chases approvals and confirmations until they land
  • Fires webhooks and updates records automatically
  • Flags anything that falls outside the rules
  • Keeps a full, auditable log of every action

Questions

Good to know

What can actually trigger a workflow?

Almost anything: a new message or order, a pipeline stage change, a form submission, an incoming webhook, or a schedule. You define the trigger and the steps that follow.

How does the human approval step work?

When a workflow hits an approval gate, GENU pings the right person privately with the context and one-tap options. It pauses until they respond, then continues automatically.

Which tools can it read and write?

CRMs, spreadsheets, calendars and anything with an API or webhook. See the integrations and API pages for details — and it speaks MCP for custom systems.

Is there a record of what it did?

Yes. Every action is written to an audit log with a timestamp, the trigger, the result and any human sign-off — so operations stays fully accountable.
