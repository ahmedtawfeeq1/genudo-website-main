Can it handle bookings and changes directly?

Yes — it works against your real availability and policies to handle reservations, changes and cancellations, syncing to your PMS or booking tool via integrations.

Will it stay accurate on rates and policies?

It answers only from your rates, policies and curated local info, and avoids overpromising on edge cases — routing anything it isn’t sure of to your team.

Does it work in multiple languages?

Yes. It detects and replies in the guest’s language while keeping your brand voice consistent across every channel.

Can it support guests during their stay?

Absolutely — before, during and after. It handles in-stay requests and post-stay follow-ups, and flags VIPs to your front office.
