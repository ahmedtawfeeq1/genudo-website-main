Good to know

Can it take registrations and payments?

It handles sign-ups and ticket bookings against real capacity and shares payment links, while the Operations Assistant chases outstanding payments and keeps rosters in sync.

How does it handle parent questions?

It answers schedule, pricing, safety and logistics questions from your own information in a reassuring tone, and escalates anything sensitive to your team.

Can it manage waitlists and capacity?

Yes — it books against real capacity, opens waitlists automatically when sessions fill, and alerts your coordinators.

Does it work for one-off events too?

Absolutely — from multi-week camps to single-day events, it manages registrations, reminders and attendee questions the same way.
