The problem

One confident wrong answer undoes the trust.

Ungrounded AI guesses at prices and policies, goes stale the moment something changes, and can never tell you where an answer came from.

Bots make things up

Invented prices, policies and details can burn a customer’s trust or cost you the sale outright.

Knowledge goes stale

Change a price or policy and every scripted answer is instantly out of date — and rarely all fixed.

Answers you can’t trace

When a bot replies, you have no idea where it got the answer — or if it came from your business at all.
