How it works

From raw conversations to a number you can act on.

01

Everything is logged

Every message, booking and hand-off across every channel is captured automatically — no manual tagging.

02

Stages roll up

Conversations map to your pipeline stages, building the funnel and conversion between each step in real time.

03

Cost is attributed

Model and channel spend is tied to outcomes, so you see cost per booking and per won deal — not just a bill.

04

Health is flagged

Response times and follow-up limits surface the conversations that need a human before they go cold.
