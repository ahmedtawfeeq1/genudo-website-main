The guardrails that actually matter for customer-facing AI

MAMariam AdelMay 18, 20268 min read
Guardrails that matter

“Put an AI on our website” is easy. “Put an AI in front of paying customers and sleep at night” is the real task. The gap between those two is guardrails — and only a few of them actually matter.

1. On-policy answers

The agent should answer from your knowledge — policies, prices, docs — and cite what it used. When it isn’t confident, it should say so and escalate, not invent a refund you never offered.

2. Human-in-the-loop

Anything high-stakes runs past a person. A human can take over any conversation instantly, and the agent pauses its automation the moment they do.

3. Knowing when to stop

For a clinic, that means never giving medical advice. For a bank, never a balance without auth. Good guardrails are as much about what the agent refuses to do as what it does.

  • Escalate by topic, sentiment, and value.
  • Scope data access and log every action.
  • Test changes against real cases before they ship.
Trust isn’t a feature you add at the end. It’s the shape of the whole system.

Get these three right and customer-facing AI stops being a risk you tolerate and becomes a teammate you rely on. That’s the standard GenuDo is built to.
